# Privacy

DealScryer stores its configuration, local scan history, Watchlist, and previous
scan in World of Warcraft SavedVariables on the player's computer.

DealScryer does not contain an HTTP client and does not transmit SavedVariables
or scan results to an external website.

The Undermine Exchange context-menu action displays a copyable website address
and item information. Opening that address is a manual user action outside WoW.

DealScryer's region/realm identifier is used only to separate local SavedVariables market data. It is not transmitted externally by the addon.
