# DealScryer 3.1.2 — Launch Announcement

DealScryer is a standalone **World of Warcraft Retail Auction House scanner** created by **Burn**.

It is designed to help players spot potential flipping opportunities by analyzing Blizzard Auction House snapshot data and presenting **profit, ROI, discount, target price, market gap and confidence** in one place.

Instead of blindly scoring unusual markets, DealScryer marks suspicious or unreliable situations with a large red **!** so they can be reviewed manually.

## Main features

- Standalone AH scanning — no TSM or Auctionator required
- 0–100 opportunity score with WoW-style rarity colors
- Profit, ROI, discount and target-price calculations
- Suspicious-market detection
- Previous Scan and local market history
- Watchlist, search and advanced filters
- Direct live Auction House search
- Oribos Exchange / Undermine Exchange integration
- Realm and region reference values
- Compact 4-day Oribos trend graph
- Minimap and Auction House launcher buttons
